[Open LLM Router](https://open-llm-router.com) is a separate service, with its own account and terms. Connect it to Octomerge, and the models your Open LLM Router account offers become one of your AI connections: they appear in each chat's model menu and to agents, like the models of any connection.

Octomerge reads which Open LLM Router account approved and the models it offers, and sends your chats' and agents' requests to those models. Your account there is set up and managed in Open LLM Router, not in Octomerge.

<Note>
Open LLM Router is offered only where your installation is set up for it. Without it, **Settings → Models** doesn't offer it. Connections made before stay listed as not configured: their requests fail, and you can rename or delete them.
</Note>

## Connect

1. Open **Settings → Models** and choose **Add connection → Open LLM Router**, then **Connect**. In the setup checklist's Models step, choose **Connect your models via Open LLM Router**: Open LLM Router opens in a window, or a tab on phones, and the checklist stays where it is.
2. In Open LLM Router, sign in and approve Octomerge's access. If you don't approve, nothing is connected.
3. Back in Octomerge, a preview shows the models. Nothing is saved yet: choose **Finish** to add the connection, or **Cancel** to revoke the access you just approved.

Approve within 10 minutes of choosing **Connect**, and finish the preview within 30 minutes; after that, connect again. A preview you closed without finishing waits on the page with a **Review** button until it expires. If your browser blocks the window, open Open LLM Router in a new tab from the link the step offers; you then finish in that tab.

## What the preview shows

- **Connected as**: the Open LLM Router account that approved.
- **Models** that will be imported, each by its ID with the efforts it accepts and its default effort, its context window and output limit.
- **Not imported**: models Octomerge leaves out, with the reason.

When none of the models can be imported, the preview says so and offers only **Cancel**, with **Manage in router** to open Open LLM Router. Connect again once it offers a model Octomerge can import.

## Your default model

The preview offers **Use *model* as my default** for the first model it imports, in the order Open LLM Router lists them, at its default effort. It is ticked when you have no default model yet, and unticked when you have one, so your default changes only when you tick it. Projects and branches keep their own defaults. Connecting again never changes defaults.

## Model IDs are unique

Each model ID belongs to one of your connections, so a chat, a default or an agent always names one model.

- A second Open LLM Router connection, of another Open LLM Router account, can't import the IDs the first one does: its preview is refused until you delete one of them. Open LLM Router's model IDs can't be renamed.
- When one of your own keys' connections uses one of these IDs, the preview names that connection and offers to rename the key's model, for example to `claude-opus-5-5-2`. The renamed model keeps sending its old ID to its provider, so it works as before; your defaults that chose it follow the new ID. **Finish** waits until no ID clashes. Nothing is renamed or dropped without your confirmation.
- A model ID you add to a connection yourself can't be one another connection uses either. The editor flags it as you type and offers a free ID.

## Manage the connection

The connection appears in **Settings → Models** with an **Open LLM Router** badge and its status: **Connected**, **Reconnect needed**, **Disconnecting** or **Disconnected**. Enable it, make it the default and choose its default model as for any connection. Its **⋯** menu opens **Open LLM Router…**, which shows the account it was approved by, when Octomerge last read it and its models, with these actions:

- **Refresh** reads the models again and lists what changed, such as a model Open LLM Router now offers or no longer offers. **Apply changes** updates the connection; **Keep current models** leaves it as it is. A model a chat or default chose that is no longer offered is refused when it runs, never swapped for another.
- **Manage in router** opens Open LLM Router.
- **Test model** sends one short prompt through Open LLM Router.
- **Disconnect**, **Reconnect**, **Rename** and **Delete**: see below.

The connection's base URL and headers belong to Open LLM Router, so it has no **Edit** form.

## Revoke access

- **Disconnect** revokes Octomerge's access in Open LLM Router. Requests already running finish; new ones fail with **Reconnect Open LLM Router**, and nothing falls back to another connection. The connection stays, so chats keep their model choice: **Reconnect** and approve again to use it. While Open LLM Router can't be reached, the connection shows **Disconnecting** and isn't used; Octomerge revokes it as soon as it can.
- **Delete** revokes the access too, and removes the connection.
- When Octomerge's access ends on Open LLM Router's side, the connection shows **Reconnect needed** the next time Octomerge uses it: **Reconnect** to use it again.

Disconnecting or deleting the connection ends only Octomerge's access; your Open LLM Router account stays. Deleting your Octomerge account ends that access too, and doesn't delete your Open LLM Router account ([Deleting your account](/get-started/sign-in#deleting-your-account)).

## When a request fails

When Open LLM Router refuses a request, the chat's agent shows a notice with Open LLM Router's reason and what you can do: fix the cause in Open LLM Router (the connection's **Manage in router**), or choose another model. Depending on the reason, the session retries by itself, or stops until you choose **Resume**. **Reconnect Open LLM Router** means Octomerge's access has ended, here or in Open LLM Router: reconnect the connection from its menu, or choose another model, then **Resume**.

**Test model** and **Refresh** say what Open LLM Router answered too.
